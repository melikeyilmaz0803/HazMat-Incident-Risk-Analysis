## Question 2: Root Cause Analysis

### Business Question

What are the most common root causes of HazMat incidents?

### Result

| Root Cause | Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Packaging Issue | 24 |
| Procedure Gap | 21 |
| Improper Handling | 20 |
| Equipment Failure | 16 |
| Training Gap | 16 |
| Storage Practice | 3 |

### EHS Insight

Packaging issues are the primary contributor, followed by procedure gaps and improper handling. This points to packaging controls, standard operating procedures, and handling practices as key leverage points for prevention.

### Actionable Notes

- Break down "Packaging Issue" into subcategories (design, labeling, fill/closure) and implement targeted checks at each stage.
- Align "Procedure Gap" with updated SOPs and ensure accessible quick-reference guides for frontline workers.
